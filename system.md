**Product Vision**

Build a **white-label Automotive SaaS ERP** for car showrooms, dealers, brokers, import/export companies, and multi-branch automotive groups.

The product is not for one company. It is a subscription-based SaaS platform that any dealership can use to manage:

- Cars
- Leads
- Customers
- Sales
- Reservations
- Invoices
- Import/export
- Documents
- Finance
- Marketing
- Branches
- Users
- Reports
- AI automation

Possible product name for planning: **AutoSphere ERP**.
